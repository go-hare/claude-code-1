### Race fixed (changelog meaning)

Without `Ye()` **before** generator cleanup, late `Ke({type:"turn"})` can enqueue while Me winds down → started then abandoned (phantom turn). With closed-gate: late turns never enqueue; Ne finally marks cancelled/discarded.
