## X1r vs frame limits (densable)
- **X1r = 1_048_576**: the ONLY cross-session NDJSON line cap used for:
  1) send refuse in `oFd` (`n = Ddd + JSON.length + 1 > X1r` → `tFd` / `eFd`)
  2) recv buffer drop (`s.length > X1r` → log + destroy)
- **No densable `MAX_UDS_FRAME_BYTES=64KiB`** on this messaging path. 64KiB hits elsewhere in the SEA binary are unrelated (Bun writers / unrelated maxBuffer).
- Auth preamble `Ddd` is budgeted into the send check even when the written wire omits `BVs(token)`.
